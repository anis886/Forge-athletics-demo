Before you step in

YOUR FIRST DAY.
SORTED.

You do not need to be fit to start. You need a place to start.

I'm new to training. Where do I begin?

Start with a coached Strength session or a first visit consultation. We walk through the movements, offer variations and help you choose an appropriate load.

What do I need to bring?

Comfortable training clothes, closed-toe trainers and water. Arrive ten minutes before your class to get oriented.

Can I try a class before joining?

Yes, the concept includes a first-session pass. On this demo, the button creates a sample confirmation only. It does not purchase a pass or reserve a class.

How do bookings and cancellations work?

This website is a fictional gym demo. No booking system is connected, no payment is collected, and no real cancellation policy applies. Reloading clears your sample confirmation.

Where is the gym?

FORGE is a design concept without an operating location. Photos are illustrative. Prices, names, hours and timetable entries are sample content.
